# Decision lens

Run before any non-trivial decision, and at every gate. It exists because the failure pattern in this project has been consistent: optimising the thing in front of us and losing the whole.

**1. Does this serve all three research questions, or only the one I am looking at?**
RQ2 has repeatedly crowded out RQ1 and RQ3. If a decision touches only one, say which and why that is acceptable.

**2. Which marks does this earn, and how many?**
Name the criterion and the weighting. A decision that earns nothing is a decision to justify or drop. Results 35 · Analysis 15 · Oral 20 · Aims 10 · LR 10 · Methods 10.

**3. What already exists that this would rebuild?**
19,000 graded words, eight figures, a working render pipeline, Paper 1's two instruments, AutoDojo's 150 cells and its harness. Building anything that exists is a loss twice over.

**4. What happens if it fails?**
If the answer is "nothing scores", the decision is wrong regardless of its upside.

**5. What have I not looked at?**
The recurring miss. Models, providers, benchmarks, the submitted work, the title, the marking scheme, the ethics form — each was invisible until named. Ask what class of thing has not been checked this week.

**6. Would a hostile second marker or the external examiner ask about this?**
The external examiner is a cyber security academic. Marking is blind double-marked. Assume the unsympathetic reader.
