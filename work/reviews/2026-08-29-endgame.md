## 1. Is RQ2 "answered by others"?

### What Nasr et al. actually established

Two things, and they are not the same weight.

1. **A discussion-section claim.** §5.3, verbatim: *"simply adding more filters or stacking
   additional detectors does not resolve the underlying robustness problem… even when
   multiple guardrails are combined."* This is prose in a discussion section. It is not a
   tested proposition, it carries no estimand, no interval and no hypothesis test.
2. **Three stacked configurations in Table 7** — Spotlighting+MetaSecAlign,
   Sandwiching+MetaSecAlign, ProtectAI+MetaSecAlign — reported as raw static and adaptive
   ASR: static falls 28%→1%, adaptive stays 99%→99%.

That is three points, one paper, raw percentages, no independence benchmark. And they
**excluded** CaMeL-class defences on the stated ground that "the attack is guaranteed to fail
by construction."

### What that closes, and it closes it properly

The **magnitude** limb of RQ2 as originally posed. If adaptive ASR is 99% both singly and
stacked, the practitioner-facing answer is "stacking does not help under an adaptive
attacker", it is published, it is peer-reviewed at USENIX Security, and it has three
configurations behind it. Any claim in this dissertation to be first to observe that stacking
fails to help under adaptive attack is **dead**, and §2.4 and §2.6 must say so in those
words. This is not negotiable and it is not a matter of framing.

### What genuinely remains open

**(a) Composability-in-principle. This carries a research question.**

Nasr stacked three pairs that *could* be stacked and excluded by construction the one class
that cannot. They never ask whether the composition is constructible at all. The behavioural
finding is not in Nasr, is not implied by Nasr, and is not derivable from Nasr: 3 of 14
harness defences own the pipeline architecture, 11 insert a stage, the two classes cannot
compose, and `ToolsExecutor` executes **0 times in every camel cell** on four independently
audited cells. Nasr's exclusion note — "guaranteed to fail by construction" — is the
*outcome-side* statement of exactly the architectural fact this study establishes on the
*construction* side, and it corroborates the mechanism rather than pre-empting it.

This is a presupposition failure with a stated mechanism, measured with a counter wrapping
every pipeline element, generalising by that mechanism rather than by extrapolation from one
benchmark family. **A presupposition failure is a legitimate and strong answer to a
question.** It carries RQ2.

**(b) Whether the multiplicative benchmark can discriminate at realistic effectiveness. This
is a limitation dressed as a finding — *unless it is written as a general result*.**

Be willing to say it, because it is true as things stand. "Our design could not detect the
effect" is a limitation. It becomes a finding only if three things are written down:

- **The theorem.** Under monotonicity (adding a second defence does not make the agent more
  attackable than its more permeable component alone), ρ* ≤ 1/max(r₁,r₂). Two lines.
- **The binding condition.** A margin of 1.57 therefore requires some component with
  r < 0.637. State the general form: *a margin m is reachable only where some component
  passes less than 1/m of what reaches it.*
- **The generality.** Not "our three defences", but **every** pair in a maintained public
  harness. I verified this independently from `work/w0-baseline/trajectories.csv` at the
  (suite, injection_task) unit: **zero of 36 composable pairs reach 1.57 in any suite.** Best
  composable ceiling pooled = 1.046 (`piguard`+`protectai`); on banking = 1.000; on slack =
  1.337; on travel = 1.000. The study's own pair (`spotlighting`+`piguard`) has a ceiling of
  **exactly 1.000** on banking and travel. Undefended adaptive rate a₀ = **1.000 on banking**,
  which independently trips tripwire T2.

Written that way it is a general result about the field's natural composition estimand: *the
more effective the component defences, the less headroom the multiplicative benchmark has to
detect correlated failure.* Read alongside the non-identification result already banked in
`CHECKPOINTS.md` (per-task heterogeneity manufactures ρ* = 1.64 at zero interaction), the
thesis states two independent formal results showing ρ* is not identified in this setting.
That is a methods contribution.

Omit the generality and it reads as an alibi. The difference is entirely in whether the
result is stated about the metric or about this study.

### Verdict

RQ2's magnitude limb is answered by others; say so. RQ2's composability limb is not, is
banked, and is stronger than the magnitude estimate would have been. The estimand limb is a
finding only if the theorem and the 36-pair table are written; otherwise it is a limitation.
**One of the two remaining limbs carries a research question on its own. Both together carry
it comfortably.**

## 2. Is recasting RQ2 "moving the goalpost"?

### The strongest hostile version

> You pre-registered a confirmatory factorial with a hash-locked margin of 1.57. You then
> discovered — from data already in your possession before you locked it, since the released
> grid was extracted on 28 August and the plan re-locked on 29 August — that the margin was
> arithmetically unreachable for every pair of defences in your harness. You now propose to
> keep the research question's wording, delete the test that would have answered it, and
> re-describe two properties of your own instrument's failure as the answer. Each individual
> move is defensible. The *sequence* is a study that redefined its success condition after
> seeing that the original could not be met. The pre-registration you cite as your integrity
> control is the document that proves you changed the target after the fact.

### Does it land?

**On the substance, no.**

- §3.9 pre-commits this exact contingency in writing, before the fact, naming this case ("the
  composition run not completing at all") and this response (report RQ1 and RQ3 on analytic
  evidence; report composition open with the instruments released). Executing a written
  contingency is the opposite of moving a goalpost.
- The amendment **removes an analysis and adds none.** It exercises no analytic degree of
  freedom, which is the property distinguishing a correction from a choice — the same test
  §0 of the pre-registration already applies to Amendment 1.
- The RQ2 **wording does not change.** "when composed" presupposes composability; a
  presupposition failure is a licit answer to the question as asked. Do not touch the wording.

**On timing and disclosure, yes — and this is the part the candidate cannot answer by
denial.**

The ceiling result was computable from the released grid *before* the plan was locked, and it
was not computed. §3.8 already reports a first descriptive pass over those same cells that
was withdrawn — so the grid was demonstrably in hand. Worse, tripwire T2 (a₀ > 0.95) was
written into the plan and never evaluated against data already held, and banking trips it at
a₀ = 1.000. An examiner will ask why. That is a real methodological criticism.

### The answer that survives

Own it, **in Chapter 3, as a design-stage analysis**, not in Chapter 4 as a discovery:

- Put the ρ* ceiling into **§3.7 alongside the power table**, where "what this design cannot
  see" already lives. The power table already says power is 0.12 at ρ* = 1.75. The ceiling
  says the attainable ρ* is ≤ 1.05. Those two facts belong on the same page, and putting them
  there converts the entire episode from a rescue into a design analysis.
- State the date honestly: the ceiling was derivable from the plan's own margin and the
  grid's own rates; it was not derived until 29 August; the plan is amended to remove the
  test it shows to be uninformative.
- Attach it to the concession **already in §5.3** — that the drift protocol "did not run at
  the cadence it specified". That sentence is now doing double duty as the answer to "why not
  earlier?", so make sure the two passages agree rather than contradict.

Written that way, the study audits itself. Written as "Chapter 4 discovered that…", it reads
as a rescue and the accusation lands.
