## CLOSED this round (verified, not taken on trust)

1. **Primary estimand was satisfied by its own null.** Tested a12 vs a1*a2; under
   the pass-through independence Ch1 states, independence gives a1*a2/a0. Under
   exact independence Delta = a1*a2*(1-a0)/a0 > 0 for all a0<1 — at a0=0.70 that is
   +5.25pp vs a +/-3pp margin, rho=1.43. Verified numerically. Now rho* =
   a12*a0/(a1*a2), null 1. Secondary model logistic -> log-link (exp(beta_12)=rho*).
   Firth dropped: a logistic remedy that does not transfer.

   injection task. Four canonical suites = 27 (banking 9, slack 5, travel 7,
   workspace 6). Verified against pinned fork. Now 7 suites = 55 clusters, CR2 +
   Satterthwaite + wild cluster bootstrap.

   fingerprinting (a naive comma-spec would silently yield camel-only).
