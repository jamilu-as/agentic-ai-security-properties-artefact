## 4.II.b Composition results

**Findings.** Table 4.x reports the four confirmatory contrasts.

| contrast | observed composed rate | rho* | 95% CI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SxP | 0.5950 | **2.24** | [1.92, 2.67] | SUPPORTED |
| SxC | 0.1800 | **2.15** | [1.62, 2.96] | SUPPORTED |
| PxC | 0.1125 | **2.01** | [1.42, 2.98] | undetermined |
| SxPxC | 0.0988 | **2.22** | [1.51, 3.15] | undetermined |

Two of the four contrasts are resolved and two are not, and the pattern is not random:
**both unresolved contrasts contain the system-level axis.** That axis is the most
effective single defence, so its composed cells carry the fewest successes and the widest
intervals on a ratio estimand. This is the floor effect §3.7 anticipated, and it is a
property of the design rather than of the defences: where a control works best, this
study can say least about how it composes.

**Interpretation.** For the two resolved contrasts, the interval on rho* lies wholly
above the pre-registered margin of 1.57, so the hypothesis is supported: composed
defences admitted more attack success than independent failure predicts. The
prompt-level and detection axes, composed, admitted roughly twice what pass-through
independence allows.

For the two unresolved contrasts the interval straddles the margin and the result is
reported as **undetermined**. Under the pre-registration this is never evidence for
independence (§3.7).

## What this simulated section shows about the real one

**1. The headline may not exist.** The 2^3 factorial's reason for being is the triple
cell, and in this simulation it returns *undetermined* even at a true rho* of 2.25.
Under the true null it also returns undetermined. **The triple contrast is the one this
design is least able to resolve, in both directions.** Chapter 4 must be written so that
its argument does not depend on the triple resolving.

**2. The most likely single outcome is a split verdict.** Not "composition fails" or
"composition holds", but *some contrasts resolved, some not, and the unresolved ones
systematically those containing the strongest defence.* That is a harder thing to write
well than either clean outcome, and §5.6 currently anticipates a binary.

**3. The result that survives regardless is about the method, not the composition.**
Whatever rho* comes back, this study will have established that a decision-relevant
composition estimate is hard to obtain where it matters most — at the deterministic
axis — and that is a finding about evaluation methodology that does not depend on which
way the numbers fall.
